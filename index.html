The Residence

An Exceptional Offering in Polo Ranch

Nestled within Scotts Valley's coveted Polo Ranch community, this extraordinary residence presents a rare convergence of modern sophistication, artisan craftsmanship, and the natural splendor of the Santa Cruz Mountains.

The Home

Spanning over 3,360 square feet of beautifully curated living space, this four-bedroom, four-bathroom residence occupies one of the community's premier lots -- graced with the privacy of just a single neighboring home. Built in 2022 and thoughtfully enhanced with over $350,000 in owner-invested upgrades, every detail reflects an uncommon commitment to quality and intentional design.

The Kitchen & Great Room

At the heart of the home, a chef's kitchen anchors the expansive open floor plan with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, a generous walk-in pantry, and seamless flow into the great room. A gas fireplace creates a natural gathering point, while walls of light animate the space throughout the day. The main level also features a private bedroom with an en suite full bathroom -- ideal for guests or multigenerational living -- along with a dedicated enclosed office appointed with bespoke reclaimed black walnut and acoustic glass doors, handcrafted by Santa Cruz artisan Truman Designs.

The Primary Suite & Upper Level

Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite offers a tranquil retreat with a spacious walk-in closet and spa-inspired bath designed for daily indulgence. Two additional bedrooms share a full bathroom, while an oversized loft and a generously proportioned bonus room provide exceptional flexibility -- whether as a media lounge, creative studio, or children's play area.

The Upgrades

The level of finish throughout this home speaks for itself. Premium hardwood flooring, motorized blinds, an epoxy-finished three-car tandem garage and California Room, and custom garage cabinetry are just the beginning. A fully permitted retaining wall creates approximately 500 additional square feet of usable backyard space -- a transformative improvement that redefines the outdoor experience. Central heating is supplemented by solar panels, and central air conditioning ensures year-round comfort.

The Grounds

A Living Landscape

The outdoor experience at 300 Village Lane is nothing short of extraordinary. A professionally designed edible permaculture landscape transforms the grounds into a true one-of-a-kind retreat -- abundant with fruit trees, berries, herbs, seasonal flowers, and richly restored living soil that nourishes the ecosystem from the ground up.

A magnificent heritage oak, estimated at over 300 years old, anchors the property with timeless grace. The lot borders a private trailhead providing direct access to over 100 acres of permanently protected open space, where peaceful walks beneath towering redwoods reveal sweeping panoramas of the Monterey Bay.

A fully permitted retaining wall creates approximately 500 square feet of additional usable backyard space -- an inspired addition that extends the living experience seamlessly outdoors.
