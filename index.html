The Residence

An Exceptional Offering in Polo Ranch

The Home

Nestled within Scotts Valley's most sought-after enclave, 300 Village Lane represents a rare convergence of modern architecture, thoughtful craftsmanship, and an extraordinary natural setting. Spanning over 3,360 square feet across two meticulously appointed levels, this 2022-built residence occupies one of Polo Ranch's premier lots -- bordered by just a single neighboring home and opening directly onto protected open space.

The expansive great room anchors the main level, where soaring ceilings and abundant natural light frame a gas fireplace that serves as the room's quiet centerpiece. Premium hardwood flooring flows throughout, complemented by motorized blinds that adjust at the touch of a button.

The Kitchen

Designed for those who take cooking seriously, the chef's kitchen pairs stainless steel professional-grade appliances with granite countertops and a generous walk-in pantry. The open floor plan ensures the kitchen remains connected to the home's social heart -- the great room and dining areas flow together without interruption.

The Office

A dedicated enclosed office stands apart from the home's living spaces, fitted with bespoke reclaimed black walnut and acoustic glass doors handcrafted by Santa Cruz artisan Truman Designs. It is a workspace as considered as the rest of the home -- quiet, beautiful, and built to last.

“More than $350,000 in owner upgrades transform this already exceptional home into something truly singular.”

Upstairs

The upper level is a private retreat. The luxurious primary suite features a generous walk-in closet and a spa-inspired bath with premium fixtures. Two additional well-proportioned bedrooms share a full bath, while an oversized loft and a generous bonus room offer flexible space for a home theater, studio, playroom, or second home office.

The Upgrades

More than $350,000 in owner enhancements elevate every dimension of this home. Premium hardwood flooring and motorized window treatments run throughout. The three-car tandem garage features an epoxy-finished floor and custom cabinetry. A fully permitted retaining wall adds approximately 500 square feet of usable backyard space. The California Room -- an indoor-outdoor living area with its own epoxy-finished floor -- extends the entertaining footprint into the landscape beyond.

The Location

Minutes from top-rated schools, Highway 17, and a Silicon Valley commuter shuttle, this address delivers a rare combination of mountain-town serenity and professional accessibility. Santa Cruz's beaches sit just fifteen minutes away. The property borders a private trailhead to over 100 acres of protected open space -- step off the patio and into the forest.

The Grounds

A Living Landscape

The grounds of 300 Village Lane were transformed through a professionally designed edible permaculture landscape. Fruit trees, berry bushes, culinary herbs, and pollinator-friendly flowers grow from restored living soil -- a garden that nourishes as beautifully as it blooms.

At the rear of the property, a majestic 300+ year-old heritage oak stands sentinel, its canopy framing the private trailhead that opens onto over 100 acres of protected open space. A fully permitted retaining wall creates approximately 500 square feet of additional usable backyard, extending the living experience seamlessly outdoors.
